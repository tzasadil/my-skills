# RESOURCES.md Format

Curated trusted sources. Knowledge for explainers comes from here, not parametric guesses. Wisdom comes from the communities listed here.

## Structure

```md
# {Topic} Resources

## Knowledge

- [Book: _The Science and Practice of Strength Training_ by Zatsiorsky & Kraemer](https://example.com)
  Foundational text on programming and adaptation. Use for: anything to do with periodisation, recovery, intensity zones.
- [Article: "How Much Should I Train?" by Greg Nuckols (Stronger By Science)](https://example.com)
  Evidence-based review of volume landmarks. Use for: weekly set targets per muscle group.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [r/weightroom](https://reddit.com/r/weightroom)
  High-signal subreddit, moderated against bro-science. Use for: programme critique, plateau troubleshooting.
- Local: Tuesday strength class at {gym name}
  Use for: real-time coaching feedback on lifts.
```

## Rules

- **High-trust only.** Primary sources, recognised experts, peer-reviewed work, communities with strong moderation. Marketing dressed as education -> leave it out.
- **Annotate every entry.** Bare link is useless in three months. One line: what it covers and when to reach for it.
- **Group by Knowledge / Wisdom.** Mirrors [SKILL.md](./SKILL.md). A resource may appear in only one group.
- **Surface gaps.** No good resource for an area the mission needs -> `## Gaps`. Drives future search.
- **Prune.** Wrong, shallow, or off-mission -> remove, not bury. Five sharp sources beat thirty mediocre ones.
- **Record community preferences.** User opted out of communities -> note it here so future sessions do not keep proposing them.
